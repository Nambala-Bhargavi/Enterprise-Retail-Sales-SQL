# Business Insights and Recommendations (from the SQL reports)

## Insights
1. **East is the strongest region**: ₹53.65 lakh, 31.2% of revenue. Central is the weakest at 13.6%.
2. **Sales grow strongly through the year**: monthly revenue rises from ₹5.6 lakh in January to a peak of ₹26.0 lakh in November. October to December alone brings 42% of annual revenue.
3. **March is a sharp dip**: revenue fell 60% against February.
4. **Electronics drives the business**: 55.8% of revenue but only 24.6% margin. Apparel has the best margin (51.0%) and Furniture is strong at 37.3%.
5. **Top profit product is the Laptop 15 inch** (₹10.1 lakh profit). Cooking Oil has the thinnest margin (19.2%).
6. **Two products never sold**: Iron Box and Air Cooler.
7. **Retail customers bring 62% of revenue** and spend more per customer (₹3.58 lakh) than Corporate (₹2.74 lakh) or Wholesale (₹2.29 lakh).
8. **Cancellations and returns** are about 6% of all orders (13 cancelled, 11 returned).
9. **Best sales rep by revenue**: Ananya Iyer (₹25.7 lakh).

## Business risks
* Heavy dependence on Electronics and on the Q4 season.
* One region (East) carries almost a third of revenue.
* Low-margin groceries and cooking oil.
* 5 registered customers have never bought anything.

## Recommendations
1. Run targeted promotions in Central and North to reduce dependence on East.
2. Push Apparel and Furniture (higher margin) with bundles next to Electronics.
3. Investigate the March dip (stock-outs, pricing or seasonality) and plan inventory for the October to December peak.
4. Review or discontinue Iron Box and Air Cooler, or relaunch them with promotion.
5. Reach out to the 5 inactive customers with an introductory offer.
6. Investigate the reasons behind cancellations and returns.
7. Connect `v_sales_detail` to Power BI / Tableau in later tasks for live dashboards.
